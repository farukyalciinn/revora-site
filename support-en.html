Something wrong? Write to revore46rush@gmail.com with your device model and operating system version.

A pack I bought is missing.
Tap Settings › Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the store account you downloaded the app with.

Why did my coins go down?
If you go more than 20% over the 140 km/h limit, speed cameras and police radar give a fine (20–75 coins).

The screen does not rotate.
The game plays in landscape only; turn your phone sideways.

No sound.
Make sure your phone is not on silent and check the volume levels in Settings.
