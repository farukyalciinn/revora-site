Privacy PolicyRevore 46 - İstanbul Rush

In short: Revore 46 - İstanbul Rush does not collect your personal data.

Stays on your device. Your garage, coin balance, missions, records, settings and purchase records are stored only on this device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Account. There is no account or sign-in. The app does not ask for or use your name, e-mail, location, contacts, camera, microphone or advertising identifier.

Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by the store you downloaded the app from. Payment and card details never reach us; that store's privacy policy applies. Permanent packs can be moved to another device with "Restore purchases".

Analytics and ads. There are no analytics tools, tracking or ad networks. If this ever changes, this page will be updated first and you will be asked for consent where required.

Children. The app does not collect personal data from children.
